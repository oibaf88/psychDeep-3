

## Predictive remodel requirements (2026-10-16; proposal awaiting clinical/legal approval)

The core roadmap must include two distinct deep-learning research tracks plus human care coordination; do not describe the app as having validated prediction until evidence gates pass.

- **Track A — outpatient deterioration:** define a clinician-approved observable outcome and prediction time first; then benchmark calibrated clinical/statistical baselines against RETAIN/GRU-D (or GRU with explicit time deltas and missingness) and a compact irregular-time Transformer. Consider BEHRT/Hi-BEHRT-style transfer only if cohort size, target, terminology, language and licensing support it. Horizons such as 14–28 days are research hypotheses, not product claims.
- **Track B — digital/behavioral change:** begin with consented structured EMA/check-ins and diary; compare structured-only vs approved text encoder, a temporal aggregator with irregular time embeddings, person-specific baseline and explicit missingness; test late fusion only after each modality is evaluated separately. 48–72-hour alerts are hypotheses, not promises. Passive sensing (GPS, keyboard, contacts, third-party content) is not enabled by default.
- **Track C — care operations:** support a human-owned task/review queue with rationale, approved urgency rules, professional competencies, assignments, availability/caseload, due dates, feedback and audit. Never deny or reduce care because of absent data, a low score, or model uncertainty. Deterministic crisis/safety logic remains independent.
- **Scientific gates:** label definition, index time, censoring, prediction horizon and action pathway; aggregate data sufficiency audit; patient/time-separated splits, rolling temporal evaluation, locked test set and external validation where feasible; no post-index leakage; AUPRC/AUROC, Brier/calibration, predeclared sensitivity/PPV, false alerts per patient-month, lead time, clinical utility, subgroup/fairness checks and confidence intervals. Compare every DL model with a baseline and prefer simplest sufficient model.
- **Regulatory/privacy gates:** GDPR health-data basis and purpose, DPIA assessment, purpose-specific consent/withdrawal, data minimisation, provider/subprocessor/region/log retention review, MDR Rule 11 and AI Act intended-purpose assessment before exposing individual predictions in care.
- **Storage/deployment:** current Supabase PostgreSQL schema `psychdeep_v12` is the only pilot source of truth; current Render web/API stay during the research phase. Modal is only a candidate for isolated batch/GPU compute later; it is not the clinical database. No migration, model activation, dead-letter replay, production deploy or schema change without reviewed gates and explicit owner approval.
